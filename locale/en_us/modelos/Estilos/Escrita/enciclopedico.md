# Encyclopedic (Wiki)

**What it is for:** Reference text for the GM: direct, factual and organized.

## Guidelines
- Short, objective sentences, no flourishes.
- Organize into sections and lists.
- Most important information first.

## Writing example
> Valdor is the capital of the kingdom of Ostra. Population: about 20,000. Ruler: Queen Ilse II. Economy: river trade and wool.
