# In-World Document

**What it is for:** Written as an object from the story itself: diary, letter, report, old book.

## Guidelines
- Use the voice of whoever wrote it, with their biases and limits.
- Include a date, signature or marks of the document when it makes sense.
- The document may be incomplete or wrong.

## Writing example
> Day 14 of the thaw. The miners refuse to go below the third level. They say the rock is warm. I touched it myself: it is. — Foreman Orm
