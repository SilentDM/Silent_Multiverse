# Adventure Module

**What it is for:** Adventure book format: practical instructions to run the session.

## Guidelines
- Separate what the GM knows from what the players perceive.
- Include checks, DCs, consequences and hooks.
- Use boxed text for read-aloud and lists for rules.

## Writing example
> **Area 3 — Chapel.** *(Read aloud)* The altar is covered in ashes. **For the GM:** a DC 13 Investigation check reveals a lever behind the altar.
