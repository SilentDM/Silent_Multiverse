# Narrative (read-aloud)

**What it is for:** Text for the GM to read to the players: sensory and immersive.

## Guidelines
- Write scene descriptions in second person ("you see...").
- Use the five senses.
- Short paragraphs, easy to read aloud; don't reveal secrets.

## Writing example
> You push the door open and the smell of burnt wax reaches you first. Candles cover the whole floor, all lit, all half gone.
