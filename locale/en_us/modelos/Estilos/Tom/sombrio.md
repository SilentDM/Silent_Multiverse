# Grim

**What it is for:** Pessimistic and harsh: loss, cruelty and scarce hope.

## Guidelines
- Highlight costs, losses and hard choices.
- Avoid clean endings and cartoonish villains.
- Hope exists, but it is fragile.

## Writing example
> The harvest saved the village from winter. Half of it went to the castle lord, and the rest is not enough for everyone.
