# Ironic

**What it is for:** Light and witty: dry humor, contradictions and characters who don't take themselves too seriously.

## Guidelines
- Use situational humor, no modern jokes.
- Show contradictions between what is said and what is done.
- Keep consequences real, even when light.

## Writing example
> Every year the Thieves' Guild publishes a list of the most elegant robberies. The city guard uses it as its official report.
