# Festive

**What it is for:** Celebration and movement: music, colors, crowds and opportunities.

## Guidelines
- Fill the scene with sounds, colors and people.
- Offer many interactions and small opportunities.
- Trouble can hide in the middle of the party.

## Writing example
> At the Lantern Fair, every stall sells a light of a different color. At midnight they all go out at once, except one.
