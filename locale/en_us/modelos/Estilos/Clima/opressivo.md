# Oppressive

**What it is for:** The environment weighs on the characters: heat, darkness, tight spaces, surveillance.

## Guidelines
- Use physical details of discomfort: stale air, low ceilings, strong smells.
- Limit escape routes and resources.
- Make the place feel hostile even without enemies.

## Writing example
> The tunnel forces you to walk hunched over. The air smells of sulfur and every torch lasts half its normal time.
