# Political Intrigue

**What it is for:** Power, alliances and betrayals between factions, courts and noble houses.

## Guidelines
- Every faction has clear goals, resources and fears.
- Relationships are exchanges: favors, debts, blackmail.
- Show the public consequences and the backstage of each decision.
- Dialogues and documents matter as much as battles.

## Writing example
> Duchess Merel backed the king in council and, that same night, sold the grain fleet's route to the ambassador of Kor. The king knows; he still needs her.
