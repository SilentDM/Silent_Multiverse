# Epic Fantasy

**What it is for:** High-magic worlds, legendary heroes and conflicts that decide the fate of kingdoms.

## Guidelines
- Magic is visible, powerful and part of the world.
- Places are grand: floating cities, ancient forests, ruins of empires.
- Conflicts have scale: wars, prophecies, gods who intervene.
- Heroes can change the world; victories matter.

## Writing example
> The crystal towers of Aelmar reflect three moons. In the central square, mages of the Order of Dawn keep alight the flame that shields the city from the northern dragons.
