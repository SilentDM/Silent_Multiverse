# Dark Fantasy

**What it is for:** Dangerous, decaying fantasy where every power has a price.

## Guidelines
- Magic exists, but it is rare, dangerous and corrupting.
- Institutions are flawed; no one is entirely good.
- Victories leave scars and permanent losses.
- Show decay through physical details, not adjectives.

## Writing example
> The temple of Vharn still receives worshippers, though the altar is cracked and the priest charges silver for prayers. No one asks where the children he takes in go.
