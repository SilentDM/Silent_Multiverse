# Cosmic Horror

**What it is for:** The unknown beyond human understanding: ancient entities, forbidden knowledge and fragile sanity.

## Guidelines
- The threat is too vast to be understood or defeated head-on.
- Reveal slowly: signs, patterns, contradictory testimony.
- Knowledge has a cost; those who learn too much change.
- Describe what is wrong precisely: impossible geometry, sounds that should not exist.

## Writing example
> The cave walls are covered in marks that repeat every seven steps. Whoever copies them into a notebook finds, the next morning, one extra line they did not write.
