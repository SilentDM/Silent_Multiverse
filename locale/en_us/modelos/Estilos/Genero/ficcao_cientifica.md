# Science Fiction

**What it is for:** Advanced technology, space travel or grim futures; science replaces magic.

## Guidelines
- Technology follows consistent internal rules.
- Show the social impact of technology, not just the gear.
- Use technical terms sparingly and explain them through use.
- Corporations, AIs and colonies can be factions.

## Writing example
> Kepler-9 station recycles 98% of its water. The missing 2% is docked from the miners' wages, and whoever falls behind sleeps in the outer ring, where radiation is higher.
