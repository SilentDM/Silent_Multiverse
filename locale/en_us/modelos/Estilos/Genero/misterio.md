# Mystery & Investigation

**What it is for:** Crimes, secrets and clues; the pleasure lies in uncovering the truth.

## Guidelines
- Places are mundane and full of details that may be clues.
- Every NPC knows something and hides something.
- Offer concrete, verifiable clues, with at least one red herring.
- The truth must make sense once revealed.

## Writing example
> In the merchant's office, the inkwell is full but the quill is dry. The ledger stops three days before the death, and the last page was carefully torn out.
