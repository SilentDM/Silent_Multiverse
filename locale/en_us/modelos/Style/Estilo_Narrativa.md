## What to avoid
- Do not use modern humor.
- Do not use cartoonish names.
- Do not create completely good societies.
- Do not create completely evil societies.
- Avoid simplistic conflicts.

---

### PROSE RULES AND BANNED AI CLICHÉS:
- DO NOT use the following expressions and clichés:
  * "In the heart of..." / "In the beating center of..."
  * "A testament to..." / "A tapestry of..."
  * "Whispers of..." / "Legends say that..."
  * "A glimpse of..." / "Ancient and mysterious"
- DO NOT start paragraphs with "Despite...", "However...", "While...", "Many believe...".
- DO NOT use abstract adjectives without physical support. Instead of saying a place is "grim", describe the soot on the windows, the smell of damp and the scarce light.
